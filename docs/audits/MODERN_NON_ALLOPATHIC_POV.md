This audit does **not** validate or invalidate any of the traditions on the panel as systems. It does **not** ask Bios to incorporate diagnostic ontologies from any of them as automated alerts. It asks, for each tradition: *what does the practitioner already get from Bios as it ships, what would they need to get more, and where does the tradition's diagnostic method sit at right angles to what an instrument like Bios can possibly measure?* Where the answer to the last question is "at right angles," the audit names it as orthogonality, not as a defect in Bios.

## A note on regulatory heterogeneity

The traditions on this panel do not share a regulatory status. A US-trained DO has the same prescriptive authority and admitting privileges as an MD; the *Heilpraktiker* in Germany sits under a 1939 statute (the *Heilpraktikergesetz*) with broad but capped clinical scope; the licensed ND in Oregon, California, Washington, or Vermont has limited prescriptive authority and primary-care scope, while in most US states naturopathic practice is unlicensed; the BHMS-trained homeopath in India operates under the Central Council of Homoeopathy with a five-year medical curriculum and recognised registry; the German or French *médecin homéopathe* is an MD who added homeopathic training; the chiropractor (DC) is regulated in essentially every Western jurisdiction with scope varying from manual-therapy-only (most of Europe) to limited primary contact (US, Canada); the IFM-certified functional-medicine practitioner is an MD/DO/ND who completed the IFM AFMCP and certification track; the Andrew Weil Center / Cleveland Clinic-trained integrative-medicine physician is an MD/DO with subspecialty fellowship training; the anthroposophic-medicine MD trains via IVAA after standard medical school; the medical herbalist (MNIMH, AHG, NIMH-equivalent) is degree-trained but unlicensed as a medical professional in most jurisdictions; the Reiki master and Therapeutic Touch practitioner work outside any clinical licensure framework in most jurisdictions.

The panel reflects this. Where the panel converges, the audit says so. Where it diverges sharply — particularly between the *quantitatively oriented* practitioners (functional, integrative, naturopathic, osteopathic-MD) and the *non-biomarker-oriented* practitioners (homeopath, energy-medicine practitioner) — both readings are reported.

## Executive summary

Bios's relationship to the modern non-allopathic cluster is **not uniform**, and the most important framing of this audit is to say so up front. Four of the traditions on the panel — **functional medicine, integrative medicine, naturopathy in its biomarker-emphasising forms, and US osteopathic medicine** — sit closer to Bios's data-model, biomarker-import surface, multi-signal-convergence engine, and personal-baseline posture than *any other tradition Bios has been audited against to date, including the great codified non-Western systems*. The functional-medicine ATM framework (antecedents / triggers / mediators), the biomarker-and-trend orientation, the patient-as-individual-biochemistry stance traceable to Roger Williams's *Biochemical Individuality* (1956), the longitudinal organic-acid / micronutrient / hormone / inflammation tracking — these are *institutionally* what Bios's BIOMARKER domain, FHIR `Observation` import, BiomarkerConditionPattern signature engine, and `MedicationAnnotationRepo` were built to support. The IFM-trained reader looking at [BiomarkerConditionPatterns.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/BiomarkerConditionPatterns.kt) recognises the multi-marker pattern reasoning immediately; they would have built something architecturally very similar if they were building a patient-facing observation layer. The US DO and the integrative-medicine MD read labs the way an MD does and use Bios's clinical surface the way the [MEDICAL_PROFESSIONAL_POV.md](MEDICAL_PROFESSIONAL_POV.md) audit describes; the naturopath in a biomarker-emphasising practice does the same with the addition of broader functional-medicine panels.

The other end of the panel sits *orthogonally* to Bios, and the audit is equally explicit about that. **Classical Hahnemannian homeopathy** organises diagnosis around the *totality of characteristic symptoms* — the patient's modalities, mentals, generals, peculiars — as documented through the *Materia Medica* and matched to a single similimum via repertorisation. The unit of observation is the *symptom* as the patient experiences and reports it, in the patient's own language, with the patient's own emphasis on aggravation and amelioration. Bios's biomarker-and-trend frame answers a different question. It is not a less-precise version of homeopathic case-taking; it is a different procedure with a different object. The classical homeopath gets very little from Bios as currently shipped, and this is not a gap Bios should attempt to close — it is an orthogonality to name. The same applies, more sharply, to the **energy-medicine cluster** (Reiki, Therapeutic Touch, Healing Touch, polarity). These traditions work with what their practitioners call the *biofield* or *life force*; Bios measures heart rate, HRV, skin temperature, glucose, and labs. The panel's Reiki master reads the codebase and finds nothing to use, and asks for nothing — the request from this corner of the panel is simply that Bios continue to *not* claim its biomarker frame is the whole picture of the owner's condition.

**Chiropractic** and **European-scope osteopathy** sit in a middle position: their diagnostic frame is structural and palpatory (somatic dysfunction, spinal subluxation in the older chiropractic vocabulary, articular restriction, fascial pattern), and Bios has no anatomical or palpation surface. But the intervention-event logging, the post-treatment symptom tracking, and the autonomic-response surface (HRV pre- and post-adjustment, sleep quality post-OMT) are usable. **Anthroposophic medicine** has its own elaborate diagnostic frame (the fourfold human — physical, etheric, astral, I-organisation — warmth-organism, threefoldness) that Bios does not model and the panel does not ask it to; mistletoe (*Iscador / Helixor*) cancer-adjunct therapy is the most-studied anthroposophic intervention with a publication record, and a `TreatmentCourse` surface (raised independently in the Ayurveda audit §2.12) would make Bios useful as a pre/during/post observation layer there.

The panel converges, across all nine perspectives, on three structural strengths in Bios that all of them appreciate regardless of how their diagnostic frame relates to biomarkers: the **instrument-not-coach posture** (Manifesto Principle 7), the **personal-baseline-as-unit-of-comparison** machinery, and the **CI-gated alert-content policy** that prevents Bios from telling the owner what to do. The instrument-not-coach posture is the same therapeutic-relationship stance the functional-medicine and integrative-medicine training programmes explicitly teach — the practitioner reads, the patient evaluates, the practitioner partners — and is, the homeopath observes, more *Hahnemannian* in posture than most consumer health apps, which presume the right to recommend lifestyle changes the way Hahnemann explicitly criticised the allopaths of his day for doing. The personal-baseline machinery rhymes with Williams's biochemical-individuality principle (1956) that has been the rhetorical centre of US naturopathy and functional medicine for two generations. The silence-is-a-feature posture matches the *osteopathic listening* that Sutherland-school cranial osteopathy and DO clinical training treat as a primary clinical skill.

Ordered by clinical impact across the panel — weighted toward the traditions Bios can usefully serve without overreaching, and acknowledging that some entries below are non-actionable for some panel members — the gaps and observations are:

1. **No `TreatmentCourse` entity to bracket multi-week interventions.** This is the single highest-leverage addition for the panel's biomarker-using traditions. A functional-medicine elimination diet, a naturopathic detox protocol, a course of mistletoe therapy in anthroposophic oncology, a chiropractic care plan, an osteopathic OMT series, a series of acupuncture sessions in an integrative practice — all of these are time-bracketed interventions whose effect the practitioner would like to read in the biomarker and wearable record before / during / after. Bios currently has [`MedicationAnnotationRepo`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/data/MedicationAnnotationRepo.kt) for ongoing substance context (wired into [AnomalyDetector.kt:34](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/engine/AnomalyDetector.kt#L34)), but no entity that says "this owner started a 6-week elimination diet on date X" or "the OMT series started X, ended Y, included sessions on these dates." The same `TreatmentCourse` surface raised in the Ayurveda audit (§2.12, Panchakarma) is the same need here. One entity closes a gap across at least five panel members.

2. **Intervention-event logging surface for manual therapies (chiropractic adjustment, OMT, craniosacral, polarity, Reiki session, Therapeutic Touch session).** Bios currently has [`EXERCISE_SESSION`](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L111) as the only manual-event MetricType outside the substance-intake set. A generic `BODYWORK_SESSION` or `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` event in `MetricDomain.RECOVERY` or a new `MANUAL_THERAPY` domain, with sidecar fields for practitioner type and session subtype, would let the owner mark when a chiropractic adjustment, OMT session, craniosacral session, Reiki session, or massage occurred — and let the practitioner read HRV / sleep / pain (where logged) in the hours and days following. The energy-medicine practitioners on the panel do *not* ask Bios to validate their modality; they ask only that the *event of the session* be loggable as a timestamp the way an exercise session is, so the owner who wants to look at "how did I sleep after Tuesday's Reiki session" has the data without Bios making any claim about mechanism. This is manifesto-clean — recording the timestamp is not endorsement.

3. **Expanded biomarker vocabulary aligned with functional / integrative / naturopathic panels.** Bios already covers the core panel (HBA1C, HSCRP, full lipid + ApoB, TSH/FT4/FT3, fasting glucose / insulin / HOMA-IR, ferritin, vitamin D 25-OH, B12, folate, magnesium, eGFR / creatinine, ALT / AST / GGT, sex hormones, cortisol, IGF-1, the four epigenetic clocks). The functional-medicine reader looks at this list and sees most of what they order. What's missing for the comprehensive functional / naturopathic panel and would be a natural extension of the existing [MetricType.kt biomarker block](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L142-L209): the **comprehensive thyroid extension** (reverse T3, TPO antibodies, thyroglobulin antibodies — naturopaths and functional MDs treat the autoimmune thyroid panel as a unit, and the lack of antibody import means a Hashimoto's patient cannot share their full thyroid picture); **homocysteine** (methylation marker, naturopathic / functional staple); **omega-3 index / EPA-DHA ratio** (functional-medicine cardiovascular panel); **uric acid** (gout, metabolic syndrome, oxidative stress); **GGT** is present but **bilirubin (total / direct)** is not; **fasting leptin and adiponectin** (functional-medicine metabolic panel); **DHEA-S** (adrenal panel partner to cortisol); **fasting insulin and glucose are present but the OGTT-derived insulin response panel is not** (Kraft pattern); **organic-acid testing markers** (the broad OAT panel from labs like Genova / Great Plains is harder to map cleanly because the panel reports 60+ metabolites — but the headline markers like 8-OH-dG, methylmalonic acid, kynurenate/quinolinate ratio are individually mappable); **food-sensitivity IgG panels** (controversial in conventional medicine, standard in naturopathic practice — the panel splits on whether Bios should import these but converges that *if* the owner has the result, Bios should not refuse to store it). The audit's recommendation is the smaller, less-contested extension: reverse T3, TPO antibodies, homocysteine, uric acid, bilirubin, DHEA-S, omega-3 index. These each require one line in [MetricType.kt](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt) plus a reference range entry, ride on existing FHIR import, and substantially complete the functional / naturopathic biomarker surface.

4. **Musculoskeletal coverage is thin.** The US DO and the European osteopath read Bios and find no surface for what they spend most of their clinical time on: somatic dysfunction palpation findings, range-of-motion measurements, pain mapping, postural assessment, gait analysis (planned but not implemented per [WEARABLES_AND_DETECTION.md](../WEARABLES_AND_DETECTION.md)), tender-point and trigger-point mapping. The chiropractor wants the same plus spinal-segment-level findings. Bios is not a manual-therapy clinic-management system and the panel does not ask it to become one. What the panel *does* ask for: a `PAIN_REPORT` MetricType with location (categorical anatomical region — same simplification the standard `BODY_PAIN_DIAGRAM` clinical-research tools use), intensity (0-10 NRS, the universally-used Numeric Rating Scale), quality (categorical: sharp, dull, aching, burning, etc.), and time-of-day; manual entry. This is the *single best surface* for the manual-therapy practitioners to read what their patient is experiencing between sessions. It is also useful to every other panel member, every owner, and the primary-care audit. Conspicuously absent today.

5. **Mind-body and somatic therapies are visible only through HRV.** Biofeedback / neurofeedback (the most quantitatively grounded mind-body modality, with FDA-cleared devices in the heart-coherence and EEG-neurofeedback spaces) would naturally read Bios's HRV stream. Hypnotherapy, somatic experiencing, Feldenkrais, Alexander technique — these have no specific Bios hook beyond HRV / sleep / pain. The recommendation here is small: a `SESSION_TYPE` enum value for biofeedback / hypnotherapy / somatic-experiencing as part of the generic `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` event in §2, so the owner can correlate "I started weekly somatic-experiencing in March" with their HRV trend without Bios making any clinical claim.

6. **Constitutional / diathesis / miasm frames have no place to live, and probably shouldn't.** Classical homeopathy uses the *miasm* concept (psoric, sycotic, syphilitic, tubercular, cancer-miasm) as a pattern-of-susceptibility frame inherited from Hahnemann's later work and elaborated by Kent, Allen, and contemporary classical homeopaths. Naturopathy uses "constitution" and "diathesis" frames inherited from European nature-cure (Lindlahr, Lust) and amplified by the homeopathic-naturopathic overlap. Anthroposophic medicine has its own constitutional typology (the four temperaments — sanguine, choleric, melancholic, phlegmatic — paired with the fourfold human). Functional medicine has the **ATM framework** (antecedents / triggers / mediators) which *is* a pattern-of-susceptibility frame in modern dress. Bios models none of these. The audit's reading is that Bios *should not* attempt to model homeopathic miasms or anthroposophic temperaments (these are tradition-internal constructs whose use is part of the practitioner's clinical art, not a fact about the owner that an instrument can capture); but a thin `Antecedents` / `RiskContext` surface — which [`RiskProfileRepo.kt`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/data/RiskProfileRepo.kt) appears to partially provide — *could* hold the IFM-style antecedent / trigger / mediator annotations as free-text or coded entries. The classical homeopath and the anthroposophic MD would not use it; the functional-medicine and naturopathic readers would, immediately.

7. **No fasting-window or time-restricted-eating logging, despite biomedical evidence.** Intermittent fasting and time-restricted eating are standard interventions in naturopathic and functional-medicine practice, and now carry substantial biomedical evidence (the Salk Institute / Panda group, the Varady studies, the TRE / TRF randomised trials). Bios has [`EXERCISE_SESSION`](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L111) and the [intake events](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L220-L242) but no `FASTING_WINDOW` event or `MEAL_EVENT` to anchor the start and end of an eating window. The naturopath who has put a patient on a 16:8 protocol and wants to see how their CGM trace, sleep onset, and HRV respond cannot, today, give Bios the window boundaries. A `FASTING_WINDOW` event with `start_utc` and `end_utc` plus an optional `MEAL_EVENT` (`time_utc`, optional categorical macros, optional photo URI) closes this. The CGM-using owner gets it for free; the functional and naturopathic panels gain a major surface.

8. **Botanical and herbal-formula context has no first-class field — same gap as the African traditional medicine audit, with different vocabulary.** The phytotherapist, the herbalist (MNIMH, AHG), the naturopath using *Withania*, *Rhodiola*, *Glycyrrhiza*, *Curcuma*, *Boswellia*, *Hypericum*, *Crataegus*, *Silybum marianum*, *Ginkgo biloba*, *Panax ginseng*, *Echinacea*, *Eleutherococcus*, *Schisandra*, *Astragalus*, *Bacopa*, the homeopathic prescriber recording *Arnica 30C*, *Nux vomica 200C*, *Sulphur 1M*, the anthroposophic MD prescribing mistletoe (*Iscador*, *Helixor*, *Abnoba viscum*) — all of them have the same problem: [`MedicationAnnotationRepo`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/data/MedicationAnnotationRepo.kt)'s `substance_key` is built around RxNorm. Extending the substance vocabulary to accept **WHO ICD-11 Chapter 26 Traditional Medicine Module codes** (which already include several herbal-system entries), **the AHP and ESCOP monograph nomenclature for Western herbalism**, **the HAB (German Homöopathisches Arzneibuch) and HPUS (US Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia) codes for homeopathic remedies**, and **free-text fallback** for everything else would close this gap across the entire panel. The pattern-explanation builder reads the annotation and includes it; Bios makes no claim about the substance, only records what the owner annotates. Manifesto-clean.

9. **Detoxification protocols, hydrotherapy, IV-therapy events have no logging surface.** Naturopathy uses constitutional hydrotherapy, contrast showers, sauna protocols (now intersecting with biomedical longevity research — the Laukkanen Finnish-sauna cardiovascular cohort), and in the licensed-prescriptive jurisdictions, IV nutrient therapy (Myers' cocktail, IV vitamin C, IV glutathione, chelation). Anthroposophic medicine uses rhythmical-massage and oil-dispersion baths (Junge-bath). Bios has no event surface for any of these. A subtype of the §2 `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` event covers most cases (hydrotherapy, sauna, massage, IV therapy as event-with-substance via `MedicationAnnotationRepo` extension). No new domain needed; an enum extension and a routing rule.

10. **No sleep-stage interpretation in tradition-specific terms — but the panel notes Bios is right not to attempt this.** Anthroposophic medicine reads sleep as the activity of the etheric body and the I-organisation; the four-fold human enters and exits the physical body across the day/night cycle in their framework. Naturopathy and functional medicine emphasise the circadian-hormonal axis (cortisol awakening response, melatonin onset, growth-hormone peak in slow-wave sleep). Bios surfaces sleep stages, [SLEEP_REGULARITY](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L92-L96), [CIRCADIAN_PHASE_SHIFT](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L101), and sleep fragmentation in chronobiological terms. The functional-medicine reader gets what they need (and could ask for cortisol-awakening-response cortisol logging as a structured biomarker, which is a clean small addition). The anthroposophic MD reads the sleep surface and notes that Bios is silent on the framework they use — which is the correct posture for an instrument. The audit recommends no change here; this is *correctly* orthogonal.

11. **Hahnemannian symptom-totality has no representation, and probably should not.** The audit is explicit about this. Classical homeopathic case-taking centres on the owner's *symptoms as they describe them* — modalities (worse from cold, better in damp; worse 4-8pm; ameliorated by motion), peculiars (the symptoms that distinguish this owner from any textbook case), mentals (the emotional and cognitive register), and generals (appetite, thirst, sleep position, dreams). The classical homeopath does symptom-taking that can run two hours; the resulting *repertorisation* (using Kent's, Boericke's, or Murphy's repertories, or modern software like RADAR / MacRepertory) yields a similimum — a single remedy chosen to match the totality. Bios has [`HealthEventType.SYMPTOM`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/model/Enums.kt) as a one-line annotation; it has no modality structure, no rubric structure, no repertorisation engine. *Building one would be the wrong move.* The homeopathic case-take is a clinical procedure between the prescriber and the patient; reducing it to structured-data fields is the same category error as reducing a divinatory consultation to structured fields (see the [African Traditional Medicine audit §3](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md) on this point). The audit names the absence and does not recommend closing it. What Bios *can* usefully do for the homeopathic owner is exactly what it does already: provide the wearable record (RHR, HRV, sleep, temperature trends) that the prescriber can use as *one* corroborating channel alongside their case-take. The BHMS practitioner on the panel notes that the wearable record is occasionally useful for cross-checking patient-reported sleep against actually-recorded sleep, and for catching skin-temperature trends the patient hadn't noticed.

12. **Energy-medicine practitioners ask for nothing and that is appropriate.** Reiki, Therapeutic Touch (the Krieger / Kunz lineage), Healing Touch, polarity therapy — these traditions work with what their practitioners describe as the *biofield* / *life-force* / *prana* / *chi*. Bios measures heart rate, HRV, skin temperature, glucose, and labs. The instruments do not address each other. The panel's energy-medicine practitioners read the codebase and the manifesto and observe: *Bios does not claim to measure the biofield, and that is the correct posture.* The audit recommendation is preservation, not addition. Two minor practical asks from this corner of the panel: (a) the §2 `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` event surface should accept Reiki / Therapeutic Touch / Healing Touch / polarity as session subtypes so the owner can log sessions; (b) Bios's alert text should continue to avoid implying the biomarker readings are the whole picture of the owner's wellbeing — the existing [AlertContentPolicy](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/AlertContentPolicy.kt) data-first framing accidentally achieves this and should be preserved deliberately. No further integration is needed or appropriate.

The remainder of this audit walks the strengths, the gaps, and the orthogonalities in more detail.

## 2. Gaps and observations, ordered by impact within actionable scope

### 2.1 No `TreatmentCourse` entity to bracket multi-week interventions

This is the single highest-leverage addition for the panel's biomarker-using traditions, and the same gap raised independently in the Ayurveda audit (§2.12 Panchakarma). Functional medicine runs structured protocols (the 5R gut protocol — remove, replace, reinoculate, repair, rebalance — typically 8-12 weeks; the AIP elimination protocol; the Bredesen ReCODE protocol for early cognitive decline). Naturopathy runs detoxification courses, hydrotherapy series, IV-therapy cycles, botanical-formula courses. Anthroposophic medicine runs mistletoe-therapy courses (typically 6-12 months, with subcutaneous injection on a 2-3x weekly schedule and biomarker checkpoints). Chiropractic and osteopathic-manipulative care run treatment plans (typically 6-12 visits over 4-8 weeks). Integrative oncology runs combined-modality plans alongside chemotherapy or radiation.

In every case, the practitioner wants to read the wearable and biomarker record *with* the treatment as context — not as background noise. The functional-medicine reader wants to see HRV recovery during the second half of an elimination protocol; the anthroposophic oncologist wants to see inflammatory markers and sleep quality across a mistletoe course; the chiropractor wants to see whether their patient's neck-pain reports (§2.4 below) and overnight HRV improve after the third adjustment.

Bios has [`MedicationAnnotationRepo`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/data/MedicationAnnotationRepo.kt) for ongoing substance context. It does not have an entity that bounds a *protocol*. A minimal `TreatmentCourse` would have:

- `id`, `owner_id`
- `start_utc`, `end_utc` (nullable for ongoing)
- `programme_type` (categorical or free-text — `functional_elimination`, `mistletoe_iscador`, `chiropractic_care_plan`, `omt_series`, `5r_protocol`, `aip`, `bredesen`, free-text fallback)
- `practitioner_type` (optional categorical — `ND`, `DO`, `DC`, `MD_functional`, `MD_anthroposophic`, `MD_integrative`, `medical_herbalist`, free-text)
- `notes` (free-text, owner-readable, never indexed for alerts)
- `pattern_suppression` flag (boolean — if true, [AnomalyDetector](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/engine/AnomalyDetector.kt) treats baseline deviations during the course window with the same gating posture as `PhysiologyState` excluded states)

The `pattern_suppression` flag is the key clinical-safety feature: a 7-day water fast (under naturopathic supervision) will produce HRV swings, RHR drops, sleep-architecture changes that the engine should not interpret as `cardiovascular_stress`. The owner says "I'm doing this from date X to date Y, suppress patterns during the window," and the engine respects it.

For the doctor-in-the-loop export, the `TreatmentCourse` rides in the FHIR bundle as a `Procedure` or `Observation` with a coded programme type — letting the receiving practitioner see what was running when the wearable record was made.

This is engineering-light (one entity, one DAO, one repo, one filter in the AnomalyDetector); it closes the largest single gap across at least five panel members; it is manifesto-clean (the owner sets it, Bios reads it).

### 2.2 Intervention-event logging for manual therapies and bodywork

Bios's current event vocabulary in [MetricType.kt](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt) covers exercise, sleep, intake (substance, dose, caffeine, alcohol, medication), and safety events (falls, near-miss falls, check-in misses). It does not cover what the manual-therapy and bodywork members of the panel spend their professional time delivering: a chiropractic adjustment, an osteopathic manipulative treatment (HVLA, muscle energy, counterstrain, myofascial release, cranial), a craniosacral session, a polarity session, a Reiki session, a Therapeutic Touch session, a massage, an acupuncture session (which sits in the integrative-medicine practice as well as in TCM proper), a Feldenkrais lesson, an Alexander lesson, a Bowen session, a rolfing session, a rhythmical-massage session in anthroposophic practice.

The panel's request is *not* that Bios validate any of these modalities, model their mechanism, or interpret them clinically. It is that the *event of the session* be loggable as a timestamped event in the same way `EXERCISE_SESSION` is — so the owner who wants to look at their HRV / sleep / pain in the 24-72 hours following a session has the data, and the practitioner who reviews their patient's longitudinal record with them can see what happened when.

The proposed surface:

- A new `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` MetricType in a new `MetricDomain.MANUAL_THERAPY` (or as an extension of `RECOVERY`), `MetricUnit.EVENT`, manual entry only
- Sidecar fields in event_payloads: `session_type` (categorical: `chiropractic_adjustment`, `omt_hvla`, `omt_muscle_energy`, `omt_counterstrain`, `omt_myofascial`, `omt_cranial`, `craniosacral`, `polarity`, `reiki`, `therapeutic_touch`, `healing_touch`, `massage_swedish`, `massage_deep_tissue`, `acupuncture`, `feldenkrais`, `alexander`, `bowen`, `rolfing`, `rhythmical_massage`, `biofeedback`, `neurofeedback`, `hypnotherapy`, `somatic_experiencing`, free-text fallback), `practitioner_type` (categorical: `DC`, `DO`, `LMT`, `acupuncturist`, `reiki_master`, `polarity_practitioner`, free-text), `duration_minutes` (integer, optional), `notes` (free-text, optional)
- No interpretation. The engine treats it as an event for pattern correlation purposes (the same way `EXERCISE_SESSION` is read), nothing more

The energy-medicine practitioners on the panel re-emphasise: this is asked for *as an event-logging surface*, not as an endorsement. Bios records the timestamp; the meaning of the session is between the owner, the practitioner, and the tradition.

### 2.4 Musculoskeletal coverage is thin — and the missing piece is `PAIN_REPORT`

The DO panellist reads the codebase and finds [body composition](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt) (BODY_MASS, BODY_FAT_PCT, LEAN_MASS), [VO2_MAX](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt), active minutes, exercise sessions, and that is the full extent of the musculoskeletal surface. There is no surface for somatic dysfunction findings (the DO's working diagnostic vocabulary — TART: tissue texture, asymmetry, restriction, tenderness), range-of-motion measurements, postural assessment, gait analysis (planned per [WEARABLES_AND_DETECTION.md](../WEARABLES_AND_DETECTION.md) but not implemented), tender-point or trigger-point mapping, spinal-segment-level findings. The chiropractor wants similar plus spinal subluxation / vertebral-segment-level annotations. The osteopath of European-manual-therapy scope wants the same plus fascial-restriction patterns.

The panel is realistic: Bios is not a manual-therapy clinic-management system and should not become one. What the panel does ask for, with high consensus, is a **`PAIN_REPORT`** MetricType — manual-entry, owner-driven, with the structure that all three traditions actually use in clinic:

- `time_utc` (already standard)
- `location` (categorical anatomical region — head, neck, upper-back, mid-back, low-back, shoulder-left, shoulder-right, elbow-left, elbow-right, wrist-left, wrist-right, hip-left, hip-right, knee-left, knee-right, ankle-left, ankle-right, chest, abdomen, pelvis, jaw, generalised). This is the same simplification the standard body-pain diagrams in clinical research use; specific-enough to be useful, not so specific that the entry barrier kills adoption
- `intensity_nrs` (integer 0-10 — the Numeric Rating Scale, universally used in clinical pain assessment)
- `quality` (categorical: sharp, dull, aching, burning, stabbing, throbbing, cramping, shooting, tingling, numb)
- `notes` (free-text, optional)
- `aggravators` (free-text, optional — the homeopath asks for this specifically; modality data is core to the case-take)
- `ameliorators` (free-text, optional — same)

This single MetricType is the **best single addition to Bios for the manual-therapy half of the panel**, and it is also useful to every other panel member, every owner, the primary-care audit, and any chronic-pain patient. The DO uses it to read pain reports against OMT session events (§2.2); the chiropractor uses it to track adjustment response; the medical herbalist uses it to track botanical-formula response; the homeopath uses the modality fields as one input to their case-take; the functional-medicine MD uses it for inflammatory or autoimmune patients; the integrative-medicine MD uses it for the chronic-pain integrative-clinic case mix; the naturopath uses it across the board. The energy-medicine practitioners use it to let the owner record their own subjective state pre / post session.

The `PAIN_REPORT` MetricType is conspicuously absent. Its addition would meaningfully reshape Bios's clinical surface across this panel.

### 2.5 Functional-medicine ATM framework — the closest fit, and the cleanest extension

Functional medicine teaches diagnosis through the **ATM framework** — *antecedents* (genetic, environmental, and developmental factors that predispose to dysfunction), *triggers* (specific events that initiate dysfunction — infections, traumas, exposures), *mediators* (ongoing factors that perpetuate dysfunction — chronic stress, dysbiosis, micronutrient deficiency). This pairs with the **functional-medicine matrix** (a seven-system map: assimilation, defence and repair, energy, biotransformation and elimination, transport, communication, structural integrity) and the **timeline** (a longitudinal narrative of the patient's life).

[`RiskProfileRepo.kt`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/data/RiskProfileRepo.kt) appears to provide a thin antecedents-style surface (family history, smoking history per the primary-care audit §2.6). The natural extension that lands for the functional-medicine reader is to make this surface explicitly support the ATM frame:

- `RiskProfile.antecedents` (free-text or coded: family history, in-utero exposures, ACE-score, genetic risk variants if the owner has had genomic testing)
- `RiskProfile.triggers` (free-text or coded: identified triggering events — viral illness X started symptom Y; mold exposure dates; concussion dates; significant losses; surgeries; pregnancies and births; environmental exposures)
- `RiskProfile.mediators` (free-text or coded: ongoing factors — chronic stress level, sleep debt baseline, identified food sensitivities, known dysbiosis, micronutrient deficiencies)

The pattern-explanation builder in [AnomalyDetector.buildExplanation](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/engine/AnomalyDetector.kt) could optionally include these as context. The functional-medicine MD reading the FHIR-exported bundle of their patient sees the same ATM frame they would build themselves on first-visit intake. The integrative-medicine MD sees the same. The naturopath sees substantially the same (with naturopathic vocabulary overlay on `mediators` — exposures, lifestyle factors, constitutional pattern).

The classical homeopath does *not* use this surface (their analogue is repertorisation, which is a different procedure). The DO reads it as useful clinical context. The chiropractor reads it as patient-history context. The medical herbalist reads it as case-take context. The Reiki / Therapeutic Touch practitioners read it and have no opinion (correctly).

ATM is the *closest single conceptual fit* between an outside framework and Bios's existing architecture. The audit notes this as a low-cost high-leverage extension specifically for the functional / integrative / naturopathic readers.

### 2.6 No fasting-window or time-restricted-eating logging

Intermittent fasting (16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, 5:2, alternate-day fasting), prolonged fasting (3-5 day water fasts under supervision, 7-day fasts in fasting-clinic naturopathic settings), and time-restricted eating are standard interventions across naturopathic, functional, and integrative practice. The biomedical evidence base has matured substantially: the Panda group's TRF papers, the Varady alternate-day-fasting trials, the Longo fasting-mimicking-diet work, the Mattson IF reviews. CGM-using owners particularly benefit from anchoring their glucose traces to their eating windows.

Bios has [EXERCISE_SESSION](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L111) and the [intake events](../../android/bios-contracts/src/main/kotlin/com/bios/contracts/MetricType.kt#L220-L242) for substances. It does not have a `FASTING_WINDOW` event or a `MEAL_EVENT` event. The naturopath who has put a patient on a 16:8 protocol cannot, today, give Bios the window boundaries; the functional-medicine MD reviewing CGM data for a 5R-protocol patient cannot easily distinguish postprandial spikes from fasting baseline; the supervised-fasting case cannot get pattern suppression during the fast (§2.1 `TreatmentCourse` would help; an explicit `FASTING_WINDOW` would help more, with finer resolution).

Proposed:

- `FASTING_WINDOW` MetricType in `MetricDomain.INTAKE`, `MetricUnit.EVENT`, sidecar fields `start_utc`, `end_utc`, `protocol_type` (categorical: `tre_16_8`, `tre_18_6`, `tre_20_4`, `omad`, `adf`, `5_2`, `prolonged_water_fast`, `fmd`, `religious_fast`, `free_text`), `notes`
- `MEAL_EVENT` MetricType in `MetricDomain.INTAKE`, `MetricUnit.EVENT`, sidecar fields `time_utc`, `meal_type` (breakfast / lunch / dinner / snack), optional categorical macros (protein-heavy / carb-heavy / fat-heavy / mixed / liquid-only), optional `photo_uri` (the owner takes the photo; Bios stores the reference; the photo never leaves the device), `notes`

Manifesto guard: Bios records what the owner annotates. It does not judge fast length, frequency, or composition. It does not push fasting recommendations. It surfaces the data the owner asked for when the owner asks for it.

### 2.7 Constitutional / diathesis / miasm / antecedent frames — what Bios should and should not do

This is the gap the panel disagrees on most sharply, and the audit reflects the disagreement.

The classical homeopath uses **miasms** — psoric, sycotic, syphilitic, tubercular, cancer-miasm — as a deep-pattern-of-susceptibility frame inherited from Hahnemann's *Chronic Diseases* (1828) and elaborated by Kent, J.H. Allen, and contemporary classical homeopaths (Vithoulkas, Sankaran). Naturopathy uses "constitution" and "diathesis" inherited from the European nature-cure lineage (Lindlahr, Lust, Bircher-Benner). Anthroposophic medicine uses the **four temperaments** (sanguine, choleric, melancholic, phlegmatic) paired with the fourfold human (physical / etheric / astral / I-organisation) and the threefold-organism (nerve-sense / rhythmic / metabolic-limb). Functional medicine uses **antecedents / triggers / mediators** as the modern equivalent. Naturopathy in its biomarker-emphasising forms also uses functional-medicine ATM language.

The audit's position, after panel discussion:

- **Bios should not model miasms, temperaments, or the fourfold human in its schema.** These are tradition-internal constructs whose use is part of the practitioner's clinical art; they are not facts about the owner's body that an instrument can record. Forcing them into structured fields would mis-represent how they are used (the homeopath does not check a "psoric" box; they read the case for a psoric *picture* across many channels) and would invite Bios to take a position on the constructs that the manifesto correctly refuses to take

- **Bios should provide the ATM surface in §2.5 above**, because ATM is *not* a tradition-internal cosmological frame but a clinical-history structure that the functional / integrative / naturopathic-functional practitioners use as a working organisation of patient history. The homeopath / anthroposophic MD do not use it; they can ignore the surface

- **The classical homeopath gets nothing schema-side from this entry**, and that is the correct answer. What they get from Bios is the wearable record — the patient-reported HRV-stability the modalities-and-sleep observations they were going to make anyway, now corroborated by an instrument. Their case-take remains a clinical procedure between them and the patient

The panel registers explicitly: any future surface that resembles a "constitution quiz" or "miasm classifier" or "temperament identifier" should be treated with the same skepticism as a "biological age composite" — these are practitioner-frame constructs that an instrument should not arbitrate.

### 2.9 Hydrotherapy, sauna, IV-therapy, detox-event logging

Naturopathy has a long tradition of hydrotherapy (Kneipp, Father Sebastian; constitutional hydrotherapy; contrast showers; wet-sheet treatment). ### 2.10 Mind-body and somatic therapies

Biofeedback (HRV biofeedback — HeartMath, emWave; EEG-neurofeedback; pelvic-floor biofeedback) is the most quantitatively grounded mind-body modality, with FDA-cleared devices in several sub-categories. The HRV-biofeedback practitioner reads Bios's HRV stream and finds exactly the data they teach their patients to read (RMSSD, coherence-band power, breathing-rate-locked oscillation). Bios surfaces this data; the biofeedback practitioner can use it as a between-sessions monitor without Bios doing anything specifically biofeedback-aware.

Hypnotherapy, somatic experiencing, Feldenkrais, Alexander technique — these have no specific Bios hook beyond HRV / sleep / pain reports. The §2.2 `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` subtypes cover the event-logging case. The recommendation is small: ensure these subtypes are in the §2.2 enum.

The panel notes one observation specifically: somatic-experiencing and the Levine-trauma-informed modalities pay close attention to *autonomic-state regulation* (Porges polyvagal theory framing) — sympathetic activation, parasympathetic dominance, dorsal-vagal shutdown. Bios's HRV stream is the closest instrument-side proxy for this in mainstream wearables. The somatic-therapy panellist asks not for a polyvagal-classifier (which would over-interpret HRV) but for the existing HRV display to be visible on the pull side without alert-driven framing — which the [DATA_MODEL.md](../DATA_MODEL.md) and existing `MetricReading` surface already provide.

### 2.11 Hahnemannian symptom totality is orthogonal, and Bios should preserve the orthogonality

The audit names this explicitly and recommends *no change*.

Classical homeopathic case-taking — the Hahnemannian procedure documented in the *Organon* (1810, 6th edition 1842) — centres on the *totality of characteristic symptoms*. The case-take is a clinical procedure that runs 1-2 hours for a chronic case, in which the prescriber elicits:

- The patient's chief complaint *in the patient's own words*
- Modalities of every symptom (what makes it better, what makes it worse, time of day, time of year, weather, position, motion)
- Concomitants (what other symptoms accompany the primary)
- Mentals (the emotional and cognitive register — fears, dreams, irritabilities, ambitions)
- Generals (appetite, thirst, sleep position and dreams, food cravings and aversions, temperature tolerances, perspiration patterns)
- Peculiars (the symptoms that distinguish *this* owner from any textbook case — the strange, rare, and peculiar symptoms Hahnemann instructed prescribers to weight heavily)

The resulting case is *repertorised* against Kent's, Boericke's, Murphy's, or modern software repertories (RADAR, MacRepertory, Synthesis) to identify the rubrics that cover the totality. The prescriber then matches to a *Materia Medica* picture and selects the *similimum* — a single remedy whose proving picture most closely matches the patient's totality.

This is *not* a less-precise version of biomarker measurement. It is a different procedure operating on a different object — the patient's symptomatic individuality as it presents itself in language. Bios's [`HealthEventType.SYMPTOM`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/model/Enums.kt) provides a one-line annotation field; this is the appropriate Bios-side surface, and *expanding it into a modality-rubric structure would be the wrong move*. Reducing the case-take to structured fields would mis-represent what the case-take is — the same category error as reducing a divinatory consultation (see [African Traditional Medicine audit §3](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md)) or a *nadi pariksha* reading (see [Ayurveda audit §2.9](AYURVEDA_POV.md)) to a categorical field.

What Bios *can* provide for the homeopathic owner is exactly what it provides today: the wearable record (RHR, HRV, sleep architecture, skin temperature trends, cycle patterns) that may serve as one *corroborating* channel alongside the case-take. The BHMS practitioner on the panel notes that the wearable record is occasionally useful for:

- Cross-checking patient-reported sleep against objectively-recorded sleep
- Identifying skin-temperature trends the patient hadn't consciously noticed (a Pitta-like modality in homeopathic-Ayurvedic-overlap practice)
- Tracking pre- and post-prescription wearable shifts as one input to evaluating remedy action (alongside the patient's report, which remains primary in the homeopathic frame)

The audit's recommendation is preservation: keep the existing `SYMPTOM` event as the simple annotation it is; do not attempt to build a repertorisation engine; let the homeopath read Bios's existing surface as one corroborating channel and conduct their case-take by the procedure their training defines.

### 2.12 Energy-medicine practitioners — orthogonal, and that is the correct posture

Reiki, Therapeutic Touch (Krieger / Kunz), Healing Touch (Mentgen), polarity therapy (Stone), craniosacral therapy (Upledger and the Sutherland-school cranial osteopaths), Healing Touch International — these traditions work with what their practitioners describe as the *biofield*, *life-force*, *prana*, *chi*, *ki*. The traditions vary in their theoretical commitments and certification structures, but converge on a non-biomarker working object.

Bios measures heart rate, HRV, skin temperature, glucose, sleep stages, lab biomarkers. The instruments do not address each other. The energy-medicine practitioners on the panel read the codebase and the manifesto and converge on a simple position: *Bios does not claim to measure the biofield, and that is the correct posture for it to maintain*.

The asks from this corner of the panel are minimal:

- **Session-event logging** — the §2.2 `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` subtype enum should accept `reiki`, `therapeutic_touch`, `healing_touch`, `polarity`, `craniosacral` as session subtypes, so the owner who wants to log "I had a Reiki session on Tuesday at 7pm" can do so. Bios records the event; the meaning of the session belongs to the owner and the practitioner

- **No attempt at validation or invalidation** — the existing [AlertContentPolicy](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/AlertContentPolicy.kt) data-first framing accidentally achieves this by sticking to factual data statements ("HRV +2σ for 24h") rather than interpretive ones. The energy-medicine panellists ask that this be preserved deliberately. Specifically: Bios should not, in alert text or pattern-detail screens, imply that the biomarker readings are the *whole picture* of the owner's wellbeing. The current text already does this correctly; the audit asks that the discipline be preserved as Bios's surface area grows

The energy-medicine practitioners on the panel ask for nothing else. The orthogonality is real, the panel acknowledges it, and the audit names it. This is a clean orthogonality, not a gap.

## 3. Manifesto / tradition tension points

These are *not* gaps. They are places where the Bios manifesto and the practitioner traditions on the panel could come into tension, and the audit is explicit about which side the choice falls.

### 3.1 "Instrument, not coach" vs. directive vs. partnered care

The panel splits on practitioner-centredness:

- **Classical homeopathy** is practitioner-centred in case-taking and prescription, but explicitly anti-directive in lifestyle counselling — Hahnemann criticised the allopathic practice of moralising at patients. The instrument-not-coach posture is consonant with the homeopathic register
- **Anthroposophic medicine** is practitioner-centred, with the practitioner-as-companion-on-the-path-of-illness framing characteristic of Steiner-influenced practice. Bios's instrument posture is consonant but the integrative-spiritual frame is beyond Bios's scope (and should remain so)
- **Functional medicine and integrative medicine** explicitly teach the *patient-as-partner* / *practitioner-as-detective-and-guide* therapeutic relationship. Bios's instrument-not-coach posture is *exactly* this stance — it lands as well-matched
- **Naturopathy** has a strong patient-education tradition (the *docere* principle — physician as teacher), which can be more directive than Bios's posture. The licensed-ND panellist notes the difference but reads it as a healthy contrast — the patient gets the instrument's restraint at home and the practitioner's teaching in clinic
- **Chiropractic** varies widely in directiveness; the principled / straight chiropractic school is more directive, the broad-scope chiropractic school is more partnered. Bios's posture is more aligned with the latter
- **US DO with full medical scope** practises with the same directiveness range as MDs and reads the manifesto from the same place the primary-care audit does
- **Energy-medicine practitioners** are typically non-directive in the clinical sense (the modality is offered, the patient receives or does not); the Bios posture aligns

No friction to resolve; the audit notes the variation across the panel.

### 3.2 "Silence is a feature" vs. ongoing-protocol monitoring

Functional medicine, naturopathy, and anthroposophic-medicine practitioners often run multi-week or multi-month protocols (the 5R gut protocol, the mistletoe course, the Bredesen ReCODE protocol, hydrotherapy series) where they expect to *check in* on the patient's biomarker and wearable record at scheduled intervals. The natural Bios posture is silence between owner-initiated pulls.

The audit's reading is that there is no tension: the practitioner-in-the-loop pattern is *pull-side by the practitioner*, not push-side by Bios. The owner shares their FHIR export or encrypted record with the practitioner; the practitioner pulls and reads. Silence-as-feature applies to *Bios speaking to the owner unprompted*, not to the practitioner reading the owner's shared record at scheduled intervals.

The §2.1 `TreatmentCourse` entity makes this easier: the practitioner sees the protocol bracket in the exported record and reads the in-window data with appropriate context.

### 3.3 "Personal baseline" vs. classical / textbook reference frames

Bios's commitment to the personal baseline is the first principle of its engine. The functional / integrative / naturopathic / osteopathic-MD readers read this as Williams's biochemical individuality (1956) in software form, and approve. The classical homeopath reads it as the *characteristic picture* principle — Hahnemann's instruction to weight the patient's *peculiars* over the textbook common signs — and approves.

The friction point: some clinical thresholds *are* absolute, not personal. SpO2 <88% awake is hypoxic regardless of personal baseline. Bios already handles this with [`absoluteAbove` / `absoluteBelow`](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt) on biomarker patterns and (per the primary-care audit §2.1 recommendation) should extend it to vital-sign emergencies. This is consistent with all panel members' clinical practice — even the homeopath, who treats individual constitutional pictures, recognises absolute emergencies and refers immediately.

No tension; consistency with the primary-care audit's recommendation.

### 3.4 "Evaluation belongs to the owner" vs. practitioner authority

The functional / integrative / naturopathic-functional / DO / chiropractor traditions teach a *partnered* model where the practitioner provides interpretation as part of their professional service. The classical homeopath holds prescriptive authority over remedy selection. The anthroposophic MD holds clinical authority over treatment course. The Reiki master and Therapeutic Touch practitioner do not hold diagnostic authority in the biomedical sense and do not claim it.

Bios's "evaluation belongs to the owner" principle does *not* dispute practitioner authority — it disputes the *device's* authority. The owner consults whichever practitioners they consult; the practitioner exercises whatever authority their training and licensure grants; the device does not interpose. The doctor-in-the-loop / practitioner-in-the-loop FHIR export is the manifesto-clean resolution: the owner *chooses* to share their Bios record with their practitioner, who then exercises their professional interpretation.

The §2.1 `TreatmentCourse` and §2.2 `PRACTITIONER_SESSION` entities support this — they let the practitioner's intervention be visible in the record without the device making any claim about it.

### 3.5 No "Functional Medicine Score" or "Naturopathic Index" — the right call

A specific tension to *avoid*: a single composite "functional-medicine balance score," a "naturopathic vitality index," or a "constitutional balance score" that reduces a constellation of signals to a single number. This would be the same category error as the "biological age" composite the [DATA_MODEL.md](../DATA_MODEL.md) explicitly guards against, and the same answer applies — *evaluation belongs to the owner / the practitioner*. Surface the individual signals; do *not* compose them into a single "your functional-medicine score is 72."

The Ayurveda audit made the same point (§3.4) about a "Tridosha balance score." The same point applies across this panel. The discipline of *not composing* is consonant across all panel members who hold any biomarker-using diagnostic responsibility.

**Tier A — schema additions that serve multiple panel members, low engineering cost**

1. **`TreatmentCourse` entity** (§2.1). Brackets multi-week protocols; supports `pattern_suppression` during the course window; rides in FHIR export as a `Procedure`. Serves functional, naturopathic, integrative, anthroposophic, chiropractic, osteopathic — six of nine panel perspectives directly, useful to the rest. Same entity also closes the Ayurveda audit's Panchakarma gap (§2.12 there)

**Tier B — vocabulary extensions, low engineering cost**

6. **Extend `MedicationAnnotationRepo.substance_key` vocabulary** (§2.8). Accept RxNorm + AHP + ESCOP + HPUS + HAB + Weleda/Wala + AYUSH ICD-11 + free-text fallback. Map the seven biomarker signatures in [BiomarkerConditionPatterns.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/BiomarkerConditionPatterns.kt) onto the seven IFM-matrix systems (assimilation / defence-and-repair / energy / biotransformation-and-elimination / transport / communication / structural integrity) as an alternate visualisation. No new patterns; relabel surface only, pull-side

**Tier D — explicitly not recommended**

11. **Do not build a homeopathic repertorisation engine** (§2.11). Hahnemannian case-taking is a clinical procedure that should not be reduced to structured fields. Keep the `HealthEventType.SYMPTOM` annotation simple; let the homeopath read Bios's existing wearable surface as one corroborating channel

12. **Do not model miasms, four-temperaments, or the fourfold human** (§2.7). These are tradition-internal cosmological constructs. The instrument should not arbitrate them

13. **Do not validate or invalidate energy medicine** (§2.12). Provide the session-event logging surface; preserve the existing data-first alert framing; make no claim about mechanism. The orthogonality is real and correct

15. **Do not import IgG food-sensitivity panels as alert-firing signals** (§2.3). If the owner has the result, store it as data; do not let it drive push-side patterns. The conventional / functional split on this marker is real and Bios should not take a position

## 5. Summary line for the project

> Bios sits *unevenly* relative to the modern non-allopathic cluster, and the audit's central finding is to say so honestly. Functional medicine, integrative medicine, naturopathy in its biomarker-emphasising forms, and US osteopathic medicine are **institutionally and methodologically the closest natural fit Bios has to any external diagnostic tradition** — the biomarker import, the multi-signal-convergence engine, the personal-baseline machinery, the instrument-not-coach posture, and the `MedicationAnnotationRepo` substance-context surface were not built *for* these traditions but are exactly what their practitioners would have asked an instrument to provide. Classical Hahnemannian homeopathy and the energy-medicine cluster sit *orthogonally* to Bios's measurement frame, and the audit names that orthogonality rather than papering over it. Chiropractic, European-scope osteopathy, anthroposophic medicine, and Western medical herbalism sit between, and the gaps for them are clear and addressable. The single highest-leverage additions — a **`TreatmentCourse`** entity to bracket multi-week protocols, a **`PRACTITIONER_SESSION`** event for manual-therapy and bodywork logging, a **`PAIN_REPORT`** MetricType, a modest **extension to the biomarker vocabulary** (reverse T3, TPO antibodies, homocysteine, uric acid, bilirubin, DHEA-S, omega-3 index), **fasting-window and meal events**, and a **broader substance-coding vocabulary** in `MedicationAnnotationRepo` (HPUS, HAB, AHP, ESCOP, Weleda/Wala alongside RxNorm) — would meaningfully expand Bios's clinical utility across the biomarker-using half of the panel without violating the manifesto, without forcing the orthogonal traditions to be something they are not, and without compromising the posture of restraint that is, the panel converges, what *every* practitioner on it appreciates most about Bios as it ships today.
