# Oceanic & Arctic Indigenous Medicine Audit — Bios Read from the Reefs, the Deserts, and the Tundra

**Scope:** Bios viewed not from a single tradition but from the *plural* landscape of Indigenous medical knowledge across Oceania (Polynesia, Melanesia, Micronesia, the Australian continent, the Torres Strait) and the circumpolar Arctic (Sápmi across Norway/Sweden/Finland/Russia and the Yakut/Evenki/Chukchi traditions of Siberia). Read by an explicitly non-singular panel: a Māori *tohunga rongoā* (Aotearoa NZ — practitioner of *rongoā Māori* including *romiromi* manual therapy, *mirimiri* massage, *karakia*, and the herbal pharmacopoeia mediated by *Te Whare Tapa Whā*), a Native Hawaiian *kahuna lāʻau lapaʻau* (herbal practitioner) working alongside a *kumu lomi lomi* (massage lineage holder) and a *haku hoʻoponopono* (facilitator of relational/family restoration), a Samoan *taulasea* (the *fofo* manual-therapy and herbal specialist), an Aboriginal Australian *Ngangkari* of the *Anangu Pitjantjatjara Yankunytjatjara* (APY) lands (integrated into South Australian state mental-health services through the *Aṉangu Ngangkaṟi Tjutaku Aboriginal Corporation* since 2012), a Torres Strait Islander healer, a Fijian *dau ni vakavuvuli ni wai vakaviti*, a Sámi *noaidi* practising in continuity with the Sápmi reindeer-herding tradition across Norway/Sweden/Finland/Russia, and a Sakha (Yakut) shamanic practitioner whose lineage survived Soviet suppression and re-emerged in the post-1991 revival across Siberia and the Russian Far East.

**Branch:** `feat/metric-info-sheets-on-read`

Where they converge, this audit says so. Where they diverge, both readings are reported. Where Bios's biomarker frame is *orthogonal* to a practitioner's diagnostic frame, that is named as orthogonality — not a defect to be repaired.

## A note on plurality, naming, and refusal of homogenisation

There is no such thing as "Pacific Islander medicine" or "Aboriginal medicine" or "Arctic shamanism" in the singular. There are hundreds of sovereign Indigenous peoples — the *iwi* and *hapū* of Aotearoa, the *Kānaka Maoli*, the *Sāmoa* fa'amatai social system, the more than 250 distinct Aboriginal Australian language groups (*Anangu*, *Yolŋu*, *Arrernte*, *Warlpiri*, *Noongar*, *Palawa*, and many more), the Torres Strait Islander Meriam, Erub, and Mer communities, the eight Sámi languages and their dialect chains, the Sakha (Yakut), Evenki, Even, Chukchi, Yukaghir, Dolgan, Nenets, Khanty, and Mansi peoples of Siberia. To group them under one banner is to repeat the colonial gesture that produced "Native" and "Eskimo" in the first place. This audit groups by ocean and by latitude only because Bios's region-config layer is geographic; it does not group by method.

The audit uses each tradition's own terms for itself: *rongoā Māori* (the body of Māori healing practice), *tohunga* (specialist of any of several lineages — the audit names *tohunga rongoā* specifically), *romiromi* and *mirimiri* (Māori manual therapies), *karakia* (incantation/prayer), *Te Whare Tapa Whā* (Mason Durie's four-walled-house health framework, used by Te Whatu Ora / Health New Zealand as the official Māori health model since the 1980s), *lāʻau lapaʻau* (Hawaiian herbal medicine), *lomi lomi* (Hawaiian massage lineage), *hoʻoponopono* (Hawaiian relational restoration practice, recognised under Hawaiʻi state health code), *kahuna* (Hawaiian specialist — used carefully, as the term covers many distinct lineages), *fofo* (Samoan manipulation/massage), *taulasea* (Samoan healer), *Ngangkari* (Anangu term for the *Anangu* healer-tradition specifically, the term most commonly used in the South Australian integrated-care literature), *dadirri* (Ngan'gikurunggurr/Ngen'giwumirri contemplative practice articulated by Miriam-Rose Ungunmerr-Baumann), *noaidi* (Sámi ritual specialist), and the Sakha *oyuun*, Evenki *saman* (the word from which English "shaman" descends — a Tungusic borrowing), and Chukchi *eŋeŋ-ʔɨlɨn*. It uses "shaman" only when quoting the existing comparative-religion literature where the term is unavoidable.

This audit also does not adjudicate. It does not validate or invalidate any tradition's diagnostic claims, etiological frames, or therapeutic methods. Bios is an instrument; instruments do not arbitrate epistemologies. Where this audit names a "gap," it means a gap in *what Bios's biomarker frame can usefully see and report*, not a deficiency in any tradition.

## Executive summary

The single most institutionally-developed framework on this panel is **Te Whare Tapa Whā** — Mason Durie's four-pillar model used by Te Whatu Ora as the official frame for Māori health since the 1980s, mandated in NZ public-health policy documents, and the explicit lens through which Māori-health KPIs are reported to the NZ Crown under Treaty-of-Waitangi obligations. The framework is four walls of one house: *taha tinana* (physical health), *taha hinengaro* (mental and emotional health), *taha whānau* (family and social health), and *taha wairua* (spiritual health). A house with one wall missing collapses. Bios touches *tinana* deeply, *hinengaro* partially through the [mental_health_correlate pattern](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt), and is functionally silent on *whānau* and *wairua*. The panel does not ask Bios to *do* whānau or wairua work — those are not biomarker domains — but it asks that Bios at minimum *name* in its documentation and in its UI that it is one wall of a four-walled house, not the house. This is the single most important framing change in the audit, and it costs almost nothing.

The second most clinically-integrated tradition on the panel is **Ngangkari practice** — formally embedded in South Australian state mental-health services since 2012 through the *Aṉangu Ngangkaṟi Tjutaku Aboriginal Corporation* (ANTAC), Medicare-billable under specific item numbers in some contexts, jointly delivered with biomedical psychiatrists and psychologists in the SA Country Health network, and the standing demonstration that Indigenous and biomedical care can be co-delivered without either subordinating the other. This is the most clinically-integrated traditional-medicine precedent in the entire audit corpus (more integrated than Ayurveda under AYUSH, more than TCM under PRC State TCM Administration, more than Sowa Rigpa under the Bhutanese Royal Government). The natural framing for Bios is **legible companion to Ngangkari work, not replacement** — an owner-controlled share format that a *Ngangkari* practitioner who reads English (most do; many read in Pitjantjatjara primarily) could read in a remote-community clinic setting in Amata, Pukatja, or Ernabella without an internet connection and without a Western-clinical interface.

The third specific high-relevance pattern: the **Pacific non-communicable-disease (NCD) epidemic.** Nauru, the Cook Islands, the Marshall Islands, Tuvalu, Samoa, Tonga, French Polynesia, American Samoa, and parts of Micronesia have *the world's highest* recorded adult prevalences of type-2 diabetes (Nauru >30 % adult prevalence in some surveys), of obesity, of hypertension, and of gout. Bios's biomarker monitoring is *unusually well-suited* to this catchment — RHR, HRV, weight, and where biomarker imports are available HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipid panel, uric acid are exactly the longitudinal signals that current Pacific NCD response programmes (Healthy Islands Framework, Pacific Monitoring Alliance for NCD Action / MANA) are trying to surface. The cultural framing matters: in *fa'a Samoa* (the Samoan way), in Tongan *anga fakatonga*, and across much of Polynesia, traditional body size carries status, *mana*, and family-honour meaning; food sharing is the central relational act. Bios's [AlertContentPolicy](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/AlertContentPolicy.kt) "no shame, no judgement, no nudges, no streaks" posture — already enforced in CI — is *exactly* the language register a Polynesian owner can receive without it being culturally violent. This is one of the strongest accidental-fits in the audit; it should be named.

The fourth structural alignment, parallel to the [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) finding on CARE/OCAP: Bios's local-first architecture is *exactly* the architecture **Māori data sovereignty (Te Mana Raraunga)** has been articulating since the 2016 charter — collective Māori authority over Māori data, Crown obligations under Te Tiriti o Waitangi to recognise *kāwanatanga* (governance) and *rangatiratanga* (self-determination) over data, and refusal of unilateral state or corporate aggregation. Bios's on-device SQLCipher posture, no-cloud-by-default, encrypted-export-on-explicit-owner-action sharing model lands on the same architectural shape, built for a different stated reason (LETHE threat model, personal autonomy) but ending in the same place. The CARE Principles, OCAP®, and Te Mana Raraunga are mutually-citing frameworks; the [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) analysis of CARE/OCAP applies almost line-for-line to Te Mana Raraunga and this audit cross-references rather than restates.

Ordered by impact within the things Bios *could* address without overreaching, the gaps are:

1. **No Te Whare Tapa Whā framing — and the framing is institutionally mandatory for any health surface used in Aotearoa.** Bios touches *taha tinana* deeply, *taha hinengaro* via [mental_health_correlate pattern](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt#L424-L459) partially, and is silent on *taha whānau* and *taha wairua*. The fix is documentation and a single UI surface: a "Bios sees one of four walls" disclosure on first run in the NZ region, and a [README](../../README.md) / [PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE.md](../PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE.md) section that names the framework, names which walls Bios touches, and names which walls the owner attends to elsewhere. No code change required for the disclosure; a Tier-B change is the addition of owner-set *whānau* and *wairua* annotation surfaces (free-text journal entries on the timeline, tagged) so an owner can record *karakia*, *hoʻoponopono*, ancestral-visit, marae-visit, and family-gathering events alongside biomarker readings — restoring the four walls on the timeline view without Bios pretending to evaluate them.

2. **Pacific NCD pattern library is the highest-leverage clinical fit Bios has and is not surfaced as such.** Polynesian/Micronesian populations have the world's highest type-2 diabetes (Nauru 24–31 % adult; Marshall Islands 22 %; American Samoa 24 %; Tonga 18 %; French Polynesia 22 %; Cook Islands 24 %), the world's highest BMI distributions, hypertension prevalence in the 35–50 % range across many island nations, and gout/hyperuricaemia rates in NZ Pacific populations roughly four times the NZ-European baseline. Bios already has the [BiomarkerConditionPatterns](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/BiomarkerConditionPatterns.kt) machinery for HbA1c, glucose, lipid drift, uric-acid trend. What it lacks is a *Pacific-NCD-aware* preset that surfaces these as a coherent regional pattern overlay, plus a `WS` (Samoa), `TO` (Tonga), `FJ` (Fiji), `NR` (Nauru), `MH` (Marshall Islands), `FM` (Micronesia), `KI` (Kiribati), `TV` (Tuvalu), `PG` (Papua New Guinea), `SB` (Solomons), `VU` (Vanuatu), `WF` (Wallis-Futuna), `PF` (French Polynesia), `CK` (Cook Islands), `NU` (Niue), `TK` (Tokelau), `AS` (American Samoa) region config, plus a `NZ` region config which currently does not exist (NZ falls through the [RegionConfigProvider.kt:215-218](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/config/RegionConfigProvider.kt#L215-L218) fallback into `euConfig()`, which is wrong — NZ uses Medsafe as its regulator, not EMA, and the NZ Heart Foundation cardiovascular-risk calculator differs from ESC).

3. **No Ngangkari-legible practitioner share format.** Same finding as [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.10](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md), [SOWA_RIGPA_POV §2.10](SOWA_RIGPA_POV.md), and [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §2.8](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md) — the LOINC-coded FHIR R4 bundle is not a useful share format for a *Ngangkari* working in a remote APY-Lands clinic, for a *tohunga rongoā* receiving a patient at a marae-based hauora service, for a *kahuna lāʻau lapaʻau*, or for a *noaidi*. A printable A4 summary in the device locale, with small-multiples time-series of the last 14–30 days, owner-written annotations, no diagnostic language, and an explicit "this is one wall of the house" disclaimer when the locale is `mi_NZ` is the format the panel would actually use. For the Ngangkari context specifically: print-to-PDF, share-by-Bluetooth (because APY-Lands connectivity is intermittent and clinic-Wi-Fi cannot be assumed), and visual-first layout (because the panel reading may be co-presented across the *Ngangkari* + biomedical-clinician + family triad and a number-dense FHIR table excludes the *Ngangkari* from the reading).

4. **Tikanga and tapu: sacred / restricted contexts have no representation in the data model.** Across Polynesia and Aotearoa, the *tapu* / *kapu* concept marks certain knowledge, certain bodies, certain practices, and certain moments as sacred-and-restricted — reproductive knowledge, ancestral-genealogical (*whakapapa*) information, death-related and *tūpāpaku* (deceased) data, *menstrual* state, and post-childbirth (*whenua* / placental) data are commonly tapu-flagged. Bios's separately-encrypted [WomensHealth domain](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/data/) (Phase 6 isolation; independent SQLCipher key; independent wipe) is a *partial* structural precedent for this, built for the post-*Dobbs* US threat model but applicable to tapu-context isolation more generally. A Tier-B extension: a generalised "tapu-flagged context" mechanism — owner-set, with the *category* of tapu being owner-chosen rather than enumerated by the app — that gives the same isolation guarantee to any context the owner declares restricted. Death-rituals and *tangihanga* periods, *karakia* periods, ancestral-veneration windows, and *kapu* contexts in Hawaiian practice all become safely-recordable-but-isolated, with the same wipe-and-export controls the reproductive domain already has.

5. **No localisation into *te reo Māori*, ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi, Pitjantjatjara, Yolŋu Matha, Samoan, Tongan, Fijian, or any Sámi language.** Bios's [strings.xml](../../android/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml) is English-only with no `values-*` overlays. The NZ context makes this not merely a preference but a partial *legal* obligation: the Māori Language Act 1987 and subsequent updates require government health communications to support *te reo Māori*; Te Whatu Ora content guidelines explicitly require bilingual provision. NZ-distributed health surfaces that ship English-only operate against the grain of the Crown's Treaty obligations. Hawaiʻi state recognises ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi as a co-official language under the 1978 state constitutional amendment. The Sámi languages have official-language status in defined municipalities in Norway, Sweden, and Finland. None of these are addressed today. The hardcoded English alert-text in [ConditionPatterns.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt) (six paragraphs per pattern, 33+ patterns) is the same architectural barrier the [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §2.5](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md) and [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.4](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) audits named.

6. **No tropical / cyclone / marine-toxin / climate-displacement pattern coverage for the Pacific.** Bios's pattern library is implicitly mid-latitude temperate. Dengue is endemic and seasonally explosive across most of Melanesia and Polynesia; chikungunya outbreaks have been region-wide since 2014; Zika reached Pacific epidemic levels in 2013–14 before the Americas wave; ciguatera fish poisoning is the single most common chemical-food-poisoning syndrome in the tropical Pacific (presents as gastrointestinal followed by characteristic *cold allodynia* — touching cold water feels burning — and bradycardia, persisting for weeks to months); leptospirosis is recurrently epidemic after flooding in Fiji, the Solomons, and across the wet-tropic Pacific; cyclone-related crush/drowning/post-cyclone-infection patterns are seasonally predictable; climate-change-driven displacement health in Tuvalu, Kiribati, the Marshall Islands, and parts of low-lying Pacific Melanesia has emerging documented psychiatric and cardiometabolic correlates. None of these are in [ConditionPatterns.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt). The ciguatera signature — the persistent bradycardia + the characteristic cold-allodynia complaint — is actually *partially detectable* by Bios's RHR baseline-shift logic but the diagnostic interpretation is missing.

7. **Extreme-cold physiology is not modelled — gap shared with the Sowa Rigpa panel but with a different cohort.** Sápmi reindeer-herders, Sakha/Yakut horse-herders in the *Verkhoyansk* and *Oymyakon* cold-pole regions (winter lows below −60 °C), Evenki taiga-hunters, Chukchi coastal subsistence — these are populations whose annual physiological cycle includes sustained cold-stress exposure, brown-adipose-tissue thermogenesis, photoperiod extremes (polar night, midnight sun) with corresponding circadian inversions, and a vitamin-D / seasonal-affective dimension that Bios's circadian patterns and `mental_health_correlate` were not calibrated against. The [SOWA_RIGPA_POV §2.1](SOWA_RIGPA_POV.md) altitude analysis has a partial analogue: an `ENVIRONMENTAL_CONTEXT` annotation that named cold-stress and photoperiod-extreme would let Bios's existing patterns adjust their interpretation rather than spuriously firing on circadian inversion in July at 70° N latitude.

8. **No region configs for Oceania (other than Australia) or for the Arctic.** [RegionConfigProvider.kt:199-209](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/config/RegionConfigProvider.kt#L199-L209) covers `US`, `GB`, `EU`, `CA`, `AU`, `JP`. There is no `NZ` (silently falls into the EU config), no Pacific island states, no `IS` (Iceland), no `NO`/`SE`/`FI` Sámi-specific variant, no Russian Federation (`RU`). For NZ the relevant regulator is **Medsafe**; for Australia **TGA** (Bios has the country code but the audit notes the *Aboriginal-specific* layer is missing — there is no surface for the owner to indicate they want IHEMS / NACCHO-aligned content); for Hawaiʻi the US config technically applies but the Hawaiʻi state health code's recognition of *hoʻoponopono* is unrepresented; for the Pacific island states the relevant regulators include Fiji's *Pharmacy and Poisons Board*, Samoa's *Ministry of Health Pharmacy and Therapeutics*, Tonga's MoH; for Sápmi the regulator depends on the national jurisdiction; for the Russian Federation **Roszdravnadzor**.

9. **Aboriginal kinship and collective decision-making — tension with the "owner is final" frame.** Same shape as [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §3.1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) and [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §3.1](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md) — for Aboriginal Australian *skin-name* and *kinship* systems, for Torres Strait Islander family-clan structures, for Pacific *fa'a Samoa* / *anga fakatonga* / Fijian *vanua* social structures, and for Sámi *siida* (reindeer-herding cooperative) decision-making, health choices are not made by the individual alone but in council with named kin in named relationships. Bios's individualist "owner is final" frame is correct for personal-autonomy protection but does not *model* the relational frame in which many panel members' patients actually decide. The audit makes the same recommendation as the other Indigenous audits: do not refactor "owner is final"; do add an *owner-set* "decision council" annotation that lets the owner record (privately, on-device only) the kin or council members they have consulted about a particular health decision — restoring the relational frame on the journal-event level without weakening the autonomy guarantee.

10. **Hoʻoponopono and other relational-restoration practices have no event type.** *Hoʻoponopono* is formally recognised in Hawaiʻi state code as a healing modality and is reimbursable in some Hawaiʻi healthcare-network contexts. It is family-conflict-resolution-as-medicine: structured sessions, sometimes multi-day, facilitated by a *haku*, with measurable downstream effects on the participants' sleep, anxiety, and (anecdotally and in small-N studies) cardiovascular markers. Bios's [Enums.kt:116-123](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/model/Enums.kt#L116-L123) `HealthEventType` has `DOCTOR_VISIT` as its only practitioner-encounter entry. An owner who attends a *hoʻoponopono*, a *whānau hui* over a *kaumātua*'s health, a *Ngangkari* session, a Sámi *joik*-healing or *noaidi* consultation, a Sakha *algys* (blessing ceremony), or a Fijian *yaqona*/*sevusevu* welcome has no event type to record this under. Same fix as the other Indigenous audits: rename to `PRACTITIONER_VISIT` with a `practitionerType` annotation, and add `INTERVENTION_EVENT` as a recordable event with owner-described type — *hoʻoponopono*, *karakia*, *yaqona ceremony*, *Ngangkari healing*, *joik*, *algys*, free-text, etc. Bios does not interpret these; Bios records that they happened so the owner sees them on the timeline alongside the biomarkers.

The rest of this audit walks the strengths, the gaps, the orthogonalities, and the manifesto-alignment points in detail.

The reluctance to alert on a single signal is the right reluctance |
| **Silence is a feature** | Push/pull split in [AlertContentPolicy.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/AlertContentPolicy.kt#L13-L25); no daily digest; no streaks; no nudges | Quiet observation is *dadirri* in the Ngan'gikurunggurr/Ngen'giwumirri tradition — the deep listening Miriam-Rose Ungunmerr-Baumann named in the 1980s and which has since entered the Australian mental-health literature as a recognised contemplative practice with documented mental-health benefit. Bios's silence-is-a-feature posture is *dadirri* by accident. It is also *whakarongo* in te reo Māori (deep listening), and is the discipline the *noaidi* and the *oyuun* train into. Bios's restraint is in good company |
| **Isolated reproductive database (precedent for tapu-context isolation)** | Separate SQLCipher key for `WOMENS_HEALTH` metrics; independent wipe; FHIR exporter skips reproductive by default; post-Dobbs threat model | The structural mechanism (separate key, independent wipe, exclude-from-export) is the precedent for the *tapu-flagged context* extension recommended in §4 below. Built for one threat model, applicable to a broader class of sacred/restricted contexts. The panel notes this is one of the cleanest pieces of architecture in the codebase for cross-tradition extension |
| **Restraint on interpretation; instrument-not-coach posture** | Manifesto Principle 7; the [AlertContentPolicy](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/AlertContentPolicy.kt) push-side language sticks to factual data statements rather than interpretive ones; pull-side dominant | For traditions in which interpretation is the practitioner's role and biomarker readings are *one input among many*, Bios's refusal to over-interpret is exactly correct. The *Ngangkari* interprets; the *tohunga rongoā* interprets; the *noaidi* interprets. Bios reports what it saw. This is the architecturally correct division of labour and the audit asks it be preserved deliberately |

The panel notes — and asks that the project notes — that several of these are *architectural commitments that Indigenous data-sovereignty frameworks have been arguing for over the last twenty years*. Bios did not build a "for-Indigenous-Pacific" or "for-Sápmi" feature set; it built a privacy-first, owner-sovereign, low-spec-friendly architecture, and that architecture happens to be the architecture Te Mana Raraunga, the *Closing the Gap* data principles, the Pacific *Brisbane Accord* on health data, and the Sámi Council's data-protection positions have been articulating. Naming this alignment explicitly in [PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE.md](../PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE.md) — adding a section on Indigenous-data-sovereignty compatibility (Te Mana Raraunga, CARE, OCAP, *Closing the Gap* data principles) alongside the existing GDPR / HIPAA / Dobbs framing — would close a documentation gap without requiring a single line of code change.

## 2. Gaps, ordered by impact

### 2.1 Te Whare Tapa Whā — the four walls, and Bios is one of them

This is the single most institutionally-developed framework on the panel, and the one most directly applicable to how Bios *should describe itself* in the NZ context.

| Wall | Domain | Bios coverage today | Recommended response |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Taha tinana** | Physical: body, sleep, exertion, illness, vitals | Deep. The entire pattern library lives here | Continue. This is Bios's home wall |
| **Taha hinengaro** | Mental and emotional: mood, cognition, stress | Partial. [mental_health_correlate](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt#L424-L459) reads sleep + HRV + activity + circadian as a depression/anxiety proxy. Suggestions point to biomedical clinicians | Acknowledge the partiality. Add a "this is a body-signal proxy for mood; the owner and their *whānau* and their *kaupapa Māori* mental-health service are the source of truth" disclosure |
| **Taha whānau** | Family, social, kin, community health | Silent in the data model. No relationship surface, no *whānau-event* recording, no notion that an individual's health is held in family | Tier-B: add an owner-set "*whānau* event" journal type (free-text, on-device only) so events like *hui*, *tangi*, *marae* visits, family conflict, family reunion, ancestral-visit can be recorded on the timeline. Bios does not interpret; the owner sees the timeline with both biomarkers and relational events present |
| **Taha wairua** | Spiritual: connection to land, ancestors, *atua*, the broader spiritual world | Silent. Architecturally invisible | Tier-B: add an owner-set "spiritual event" journal type (free-text, on-device only) for *karakia*, ancestral visit, *whenua*-connection event, prayer practice, ceremony. Same posture as *whānau* — record only, never interpret |

The panel's reading: Bios viewed through Te Whare Tapa Whā is *one wall of a four-walled house*. This is not a deficiency — Bios is an instrument; instruments are bounded. The deficiency is the *implicit pretence* that the wall it occupies is the whole house. Adding a Te Whare Tapa Whā disclosure on first-run in the NZ region, and adding journal-event surfaces for *whānau* and *wairua* events that the owner can populate at will, restores the four-walled frame on the owner's timeline without Bios ever evaluating walls that are not its to evaluate. This is the single highest-priority cultural-framing change in the audit, costs almost nothing in engineering, and aligns Bios with the framework Te Whatu Ora already uses.

Cross-reference: the Hawaiian *kahuna lāʻau lapaʻau* on the panel notes that *Te Whare Tapa Whā* maps imperfectly but recognisably onto Hawaiian wholeness frames (*lōkahi*: balance among self, others, spirit, environment), and onto Samoan *tofa saʻili* (the search for wisdom across multiple relational domains). The four-wall framing generalises across Polynesia even if the specific framework is Māori.

### 2.2 Pacific NCD epidemic — the highest-leverage clinical fit Bios has

The numbers (rounded, drawn from WHO STEPS surveys 2017–2023, IDF Diabetes Atlas, and Pacific MANA reports):

| Jurisdiction | Type-2 diabetes adult prevalence | Obesity (BMI ≥30) | Hypertension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nauru | 24–31 % | 60–70 % | 40–50 % |
| Marshall Islands | 22 % | 53 % | 30 % |
| American Samoa | 24 % | 70 %+ | ~30 % |
| French Polynesia | 22 % | 50 % | 30 % |
| Cook Islands | 24 % | 55 % | 30 % |
| Samoa | 18 % | 47 % | 25 % |
| Tonga | 18 % | 60 % | 30 % |
| Fiji | 14 % | 35 % | 25 % |
| Kiribati | 17 % | 46 % | 32 % |
| Tuvalu | 16 % | 51 % | 30 % |
| NZ Māori | 9 % (vs 6 % NZ-European) | 50 % (vs 30 %) | elevated |
| NZ Pacific | 11 % | 65 % | elevated; gout ~6x NZ-European |

These are among the highest figures recorded anywhere in the world. Bios's longitudinal biomarker monitoring — HbA1c trend, fasting-glucose trend, lipid panel drift, uric-acid trend, weight trajectory, blood-pressure trend, RHR baseline — is *unusually well-fitted* to the surveillance shape these public-health programmes (Healthy Islands Framework, Pacific MANA, NZ *Counties Manukau* Pacific health pathways) are trying to operationalise.

What is missing:

1. **No Pacific-NCD region overlay.** A `usePacificNCDPatterns` flag in the region config that surfaces type-2 diabetes drift, metabolic syndrome convergence, gout/hyperuricaemia trend, and Pacific-cardiovascular-risk reasoning as a coherent regional preset.
2. **No Pacific-population BMI threshold awareness.** The European-derived BMI cutoffs (≥25 overweight, ≥30 obese) systematically misclassify Polynesian body composition; the Pacific-adjusted cutoffs (≥26 overweight, ≥32 obese, debated since the 2000 WHO Pacific consultation) are not universal but the audit asks at minimum that Bios *not silently apply* European cutoffs in Pacific region configs. The cleanest answer: do not surface a BMI-categorical label at all, surface trajectory only — which is what Bios largely already does, and which aligns with the *no-shame* policy.
3. **No NCD region config for any Pacific island state.** Listed in §2.8 below.
4. **No Pacific NCD-relevant cultural framing in alert language.** The current [AlertContentPolicy](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/AlertContentPolicy.kt) restraint is the right floor — it does not say culturally violent things. But it also does not actively recognise the cultural context. A pull-side region-specific "context for these numbers" surface that names *fa'a Samoa*, *anga fakatonga*, the historical relationship between body size and *mana*, and the political history of public-health messaging in the Pacific — written by Pacific public-health authors, not the project — would be a meaningful Tier-B addition. The panel notes this is *not* the project's to write alone; it needs Pacific authorship and the project's role is to make the surface available.

### 2.3 Ngangkari clinical integration — Bios as legible companion

The Ngangkari precedent is genuinely unique in the audit corpus. Since 2012, the *Aṉangu Ngangkaṟi Tjutaku Aboriginal Corporation* (ANTAC) has delivered Ngangkari care alongside biomedical psychiatry and psychology in the SA Country Health network, in the Royal Adelaide Hospital, in headspace services, and in correctional health. The 2017 SA Mental Health Strategic Plan formally recognised Ngangkari work. Medicare billing under specific item numbers applies in some contexts. This is the most clinically-integrated traditional-medicine precedent in the entire audit corpus — more integrated than AYUSH under the Indian Ministry, more integrated than PRC TCM hospitals, more integrated than the Bhutanese Royal Sowa Rigpa hospitals.

The natural framing for Bios is **legible companion, not replacement.** Concretely:

1. **An owner-controlled share format a *Ngangkari* practitioner can read.** Same shape as the [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.10](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) practitioner share recommendation: A4 printable, plain-English (most *Ngangkari* read English clinically though Pitjantjatjara is the first language for many), time-series small-multiples for the last 14–30 days, owner annotations on the timeline, no diagnostic language, no FHIR. Critically: must work offline (APY-Lands clinic connectivity is intermittent), must be printable to a portable USB-printer, must be Bluetooth-shareable (clinic Wi-Fi is unreliable).
2. **A Pitjantjatjara localisation pathway.** Tier-B. The Yolŋu Matha localisation (for Arnhem Land) is a parallel separate concern. Both have small speaker populations but high clinical relevance in remote-community deployment. Neither is currently in [strings.xml](../../android/app/src/main/res/values/strings.xml).
3. **A `Ngangkari_PRESENT` annotation** that the owner sets when they are in a Ngangkari session, with the same `ceremonialActive` gating logic recommended in [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) — Bios records but does not push-notify during the session.
4. **An `AU` region config Aboriginal-specific layer.** The `AU` region config exists in [RegionConfigProvider.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/config/RegionConfigProvider.kt) but has no Aboriginal-specific layer pointing to NACCHO / IHEMS / *Closing the Gap* health pathways. This is a Tier-B addition.

The panel does *not* ask Bios to encode Ngangkari diagnostic frames — the *mapan* / spirit-presence reading, the laying-on-of-hands diagnosis, the spirit-removal practice — those are not biomarker domains and are not Bios's to encode. Bios's job is to be a quiet, legible instrument that supports the Ngangkari's reading without contesting it.

### 2.4 Tikanga and tapu — generalising the reproductive-isolation pattern

The reproductive-data domain isolation (separate SQLCipher key, independent wipe, exclude-from-export by default) was built for the post-*Dobbs* US threat model but the structural mechanism is exactly the precedent for a more general *tapu-flagged-context* isolation. The categories the panel names, drawn from their own traditions:

| Tradition | Tapu / restricted contexts |
|---|---|
| Māori | *Whakapapa* (ancestral / genealogical), *tūpāpaku* (death), menstrual state, *whenua* (placental), reproductive, *karakia* periods |
| Hawaiian | *Kapu* on certain practices, ancestral knowledge, reproductive, death-period |
| Samoan | Reproductive, death (*maliu* period), certain village-chief health information |
| Aboriginal Australian | *Sacred-sorry-business* (mourning), women's-business and men's-business as separately-restricted, ancestral knowledge, certain place-based health information |
| Sámi | Reindeer-herding decision contexts (which can have health implications during long forage migrations), certain *noaidi* consultation outputs |

The Tier-B extension: a generalised "tapu-context" mechanism, owner-declared (not enumerated by the app; the owner chooses the category and the label), giving the same isolation guarantees the reproductive domain already has. Wipe controls per-context, export-excludes-by-default per-context, separate encryption keys per-context. The reproductive domain becomes one instance of a general mechanism.

### 2.7 Extreme-cold and photoperiod-extreme physiology — the circumpolar gap

The Sámi reindeer-herder cohort, the Sakha/Yakut horse-herders in the Verkhoyansk/Oymyakon cold-pole, the Evenki taiga-hunter cohort, the Chukchi coastal subsistence cohort — these populations have:

- Sustained cold-stress exposure (winter ambient temperatures below −40 °C for weeks; the Oymyakon record is −67.7 °C)
- Brown-adipose-tissue thermogenesis up-regulation
- Photoperiod extremes: polar night (no sunrise) for weeks in winter at high latitude; midnight sun (no sunset) for weeks in summer
- Circadian patterns that legitimately invert seasonally — a Sámi herder in Karasjok in June will sleep at times that any temperate-latitude pattern reads as disordered
- Vitamin-D / seasonal-affective dimension with documented impact on mood, immune function, and bone health
- Traditional diet patterns (high marine fat, high protein, low carbohydrate) that produce metabolic baselines distinct from temperate Western diets

Bios's circadian patterns and `mental_health_correlate` were not calibrated against this. The [SOWA_RIGPA_POV §2.1](SOWA_RIGPA_POV.md) altitude-context recommendation has a partial analogue: an `ENVIRONMENTAL_CONTEXT` annotation that names cold-stress, photoperiod-band, and seasonal phase would let Bios's existing patterns adjust their interpretation. The simplest form: a `LATITUDE_BAND` annotation (owner-set; *polar*, *subarctic*, *temperate*, *tropical*) plus a *current-season* derivation that softens circadian-inversion alerts during local polar-night / midnight-sun periods. Same engineering shape as the altitude recommendation; different physiological domain.

The Sámi-specific concern of *joik* — the traditional vocal practice, suppressed for centuries under Norwegian/Swedish/Finnish Christian missionary policy and revived since the 1970s — is named here for context. *Joik* has measurable autonomic effects on the practitioner (HR variation, HRV shift) similar to other sustained-vocal practices documented in the music-medicine literature. Bios is unlikely to see *joik* sessions as illness, but the panel notes that *joik* sessions, *yoiking* of family members or of places, are health-relevant practices the owner might want recorded on the timeline — same shape as the *INTERVENTION_EVENT* recommendation in §2.10 of the executive summary.

### 2.9 Aboriginal kinship, Pacific *fa'a Samoa*, Sámi *siida* — the relational decision frame

Same shape as [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §3.1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md) and [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §3.1](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md). The audit cross-references rather than restates. The salient extensions for this panel:

- **Aboriginal Australian skin-name systems** (the *Yolŋu* *gurrutu* kinship system, *Anangu* *walytja* family system, *Warlpiri* eight-subsection system) — health decisions for a person involve named kin in named relationships; certain decisions are gendered (*women's business* / *men's business* as institutionally-distinct domains)
- **Pacific *aiga* (Samoan family), *kāinga* (Tongan), *vanua* (Fijian)** — the extended family / land-relationship unit is the decision-making unit
- **Sámi *siida*** — the reindeer-herding cooperative is the decision-making unit for many practical health-relevant choices (migration timing, encampment, resource sharing)

The recommendation, same as the other Indigenous audits: do not refactor "owner is final"; do add an owner-set *decision-council* annotation (free-text, on-device only) so the owner can record privately the kin or council members consulted about a given decision. Restores the relational frame on the journal without weakening the autonomy guarantee.

## 3. Fundamental orthogonalities — named honestly, not as gaps

| Practitioner frame | What the practitioner does | Why this is orthogonal to Bios |
|---|---|---|
| **Tohunga rongoā** | Reads the patient via pulse, breath, *whakapapa* context, *karakia*, and the *taonga* of plant medicines (*kawakawa*, *harakeke*, *kūmarahou*, *mānuka*, *kōwhai*, *rongoā* preparations) | Biomarker measurement is not a less-precise version of *whakapapa*-aware diagnostic listening; it is a different epistemology |
| **Kahuna lāʻau lapaʻau** | Diagnoses through *kilo* (close observation across time), interprets through Hawaiian cosmological frames, prescribes plant medicines (*ʻōlena*, *noni*, *ʻawa*) | Bios measures the body in real time; the *kahuna*'s diagnostic time is days-to-seasons of observed pattern, not seconds of HRV |
| **Haku hoʻoponopono** | Facilitates structured family-restoration sessions, sometimes multi-day, with measurable downstream physiological effects on participants | Bios can record *that* a session happened and can detect downstream sleep/HRV/mood-correlate effects, but the work of the session is relational and is not Bios's to interpret |
| **Taulasea (Samoan)** | Diagnoses via *fofo* palpation of the body, prescribes manipulative therapy and *vailaau* (plant medicines) | Bios sees the cardiovascular signature of musculoskeletal pain (HRV depression, sleep disruption); it does not see the *fofo*'s palpation diagnostic |
| **Ngangkari** | Diagnoses via *mapan* (sensing of spirit-presence and spirit-absence in the body), heals via laying-on-of-hands and spirit-removal | Biomarker measurement and *mapan* answer different questions; neither replaces the other; the SA integrated-care precedent demonstrates they co-exist |
| **Dau ni vakavuvuli ni wai vakaviti (Fijian)** | Practises *bole* (massage), *wai vakaviti* (plant medicines), spiritual frame | Bios does not see *vu* (ancestral spirit) framings; it reports physiological signals |
| **Noaidi** | Drum (*meavrresgárri* / *runebomme*), *joik*, dream interpretation, herbal medicine, mediation between the human community and the spirit world; suppressed under Christian missionary policy for centuries; modern revival | Bios is a body-watching instrument; the *noaidi*'s diagnostic frame is cosmological. Orthogonality not deficiency |
| **Oyuun / saman / eŋeŋ-ʔɨlɨn** (Sakha/Evenki/Chukchi) | Trance-mediated diagnostic and therapeutic work; *algys* blessings; herbal medicine; post-Soviet revival from near-suppression | Same orthogonality; thin written documentation makes nuanced fit-analysis difficult and the audit will not pretend otherwise |
| **Dadirri** (Ngan'gikurunggurr/Ngen'giwumirri contemplative practice) | Deep listening; institutionally recognised in Australian mental-health literature since Ungunmerr-Baumann's articulation | Bios's silence-is-a-feature posture *aligns* with *dadirri* rather than orthogonal; cross-reference §1 above |

The panel is unanimous that these orthogonalities are not deficiencies and are not Bios's to repair. The audit names them so they are not silently glossed.

## 4. Whakapapa and ancestral biomarker data — a forward-looking note

Bios is currently not a genomics product and does not store ancestral genetic or biological material. The panel notes — and asks the project to note — that if Bios ever moves toward genomic markers, federated learning across ancestral lineages, or any form of biological-sample annotation, the **whakapapa** (Māori), **moʻokūʻauhau** (Hawaiian), and **gáfa** (Samoan) concepts of ancestral-genealogical knowledge as *taonga* (treasured, restricted) make this domain *intrinsically* tapu-flagged. The historical pattern of biological samples from Indigenous populations being taken under one consent and used for entirely separate research — documented in NZ, in Hawaiʻi, across the Pacific Genographic engagements, and across the Sámi research history — means any future genomics surface in Bios must default to the strictest isolation, the most explicit consent, and the most revocable controls. The reproductive-domain isolation pattern is the precedent. If genomic features are ever roadmapped, the audit asks they be designed in consultation with Te Mana Raraunga, with the relevant Pacific data-sovereignty bodies (such as the Pacific Health Research and Ethics Council), with NACCHO for Aboriginal contexts, and with the Sámi Council. This is a Tier-C concern (the feature does not exist) but a Tier-A *if-and-when* concern.

## 5. Tiered recommendations

### Tier A — high priority, achievable within the existing architecture

1. **Te Whare Tapa Whā framing in documentation and first-run UI** (NZ region). Single-paragraph disclosure: "Bios is one wall of a four-walled house." Link to Te Whatu Ora's framework. No new code, only content. Highest-leverage cultural-framing change in the audit.
2. **NZ region config.** Add `NZ` to [RegionConfigProvider.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/config/RegionConfigProvider.kt) with Medsafe regulator, NZ Heart Foundation CVD risk reference, and te reo Māori as primary locale where the device requests it.
3. **Pacific NCD region configs.** Add `FJ`, `WS`, `TO`, `NR`, `MH`, `FM`, `KI`, `TV`, `PG`, `SB`, `VU`, `PF`, `CK`, `NU`, `TK`, `AS` with `usePacificNCDPatterns = true` and a `PACIFIC_DEFAULT` fallback (instead of `euConfig()`).
4. **Document Te Mana Raraunga, CARE, OCAP, *Closing the Gap* data principles alignment** in [PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE.md](../PRIVACY_ARCHITECTURE.md). Cross-reference [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md). No code change.
5. **`INTERVENTION_EVENT` and `PRACTITIONER_VISIT` event types** in [Enums.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/model/Enums.kt#L116-L123), with owner-described free-text labels. Replaces the implicit biomedical-only encounter chain.
6. **`CEREMONIAL_CONTEXT` annotation with `ceremonialActive` gating** on URGENT patterns and `infection_onset` / `cardiovascular_stress`. Cross-reference [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.1](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md). Same mechanism, expanded to cover *hoʻoponopono* (sometimes multi-day), Ngangkari sessions, Sámi multi-day reindeer-herding migrations, *tangihanga*, and the contemplative-retreat case.
7. **Dengue / chikungunya / leptospirosis Pacific tropical-disease pattern overlay** when `usePacificNCDPatterns` or `useTropicalDiseasePatterns` is set. Cross-reference [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §2.1](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md).

### Tier B — substantial value, larger engineering work

8. **Localisation infrastructure refactor.** Move pattern text from hardcoded English in [ConditionPatterns.kt](../../android/app/src/main/java/com/bios/app/alerts/ConditionPatterns.kt) into a localisable resource layer. Once that exists, add `values-mi/` (te reo Māori), `values-haw/` (ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi), `values-sm/` (Samoan), `values-to/` (Tongan), `values-fj/` (Fijian), `values-pjt/` (Pitjantjatjara), `values-yol/` (Yolŋu Matha), `values-se/` (Northern Sámi), `values-sah/` (Sakha) one at a time as community-led translations become available.
9. **Generalised tapu-context isolation.** Generalise the reproductive-domain isolation mechanism (separate SQLCipher key, independent wipe, exclude-from-export) into a parameterised "tapu-flagged context" that owners can declare. Reproductive domain becomes one instance. Cross-reference §2.4.
10. **`whānau-event` and `wairua-event` journal types.** Free-text, on-device, owner-populated. Restores the four-walled Te Whare Tapa Whā frame on the timeline without Bios interpreting walls that are not its to interpret.
11. **`ENVIRONMENTAL_CONTEXT` annotation** covering altitude (already recommended in [SOWA_RIGPA_POV §2.1](SOWA_RIGPA_POV.md)), latitude-band (polar / subarctic / temperate / tropical), and seasonal phase. Lets circadian patterns adjust during polar night / midnight sun. Cross-reference [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.2](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md).
12. **Practitioner-share format that is not FHIR.** A4 printable, time-series small-multiples, plain-locale language, no diagnostic interpretation, no LOINC codes. Bluetooth-shareable. Cross-reference [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §2.8](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md), [INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV §2.10](INDIGENOUS_AMERICAS_POV.md), [SOWA_RIGPA_POV §2.10](SOWA_RIGPA_POV.md). Single piece of work serves four audits' worth of practitioners.
13. **AU region config Aboriginal-specific overlay.** NACCHO / IHEMS / *Closing the Gap* health pathway references; Ngangkari-aware language; Pitjantjatjara and Yolŋu Matha localisation entry points.
14. **Owner-set *decision-council* annotation.** Free-text, on-device only. Restores relational decision-making on the journal without weakening "owner is final."
15. **Pacific-population body-composition guidance.** Do not surface European BMI categorical labels in `usePacificNCDPatterns` configs; surface trajectory only. Aligns with the no-shame policy.
16. **Ciguatera pattern.** Owner logs reef-fish meal; Bios's RHR baseline-shift logic surfaces persistent post-meal bradycardia + cold-allodynia as a candidate ciguatera signature on the pull side.

### Tier C — useful but lower priority, or with significant external dependencies

17. **Sakha / Sámi region configs.** Russian Federation jurisdictional complexity makes shipping `RU` non-trivial; Nordic-jurisdictional Sámi overlay is more tractable. The audit notes the diaspora reality.
18. **`SICKLE_CELL_*` physiology states** (cross-reference [AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV §2.2](AFRICAN_TRADITIONAL_POV.md)) — relevant to the Melanesian cohort where alpha-thalassaemia is common.
19. **Climate-displacement health framing** for Tuvalu / Kiribati / Marshall Islands. Pull-side regional advisory only; the political dimension is significant.
20. **Cyclone-aftermath pattern context.** The audit notes the difficulty of doing this without a cloud weather-API dependency that would violate the no-cloud-by-default posture. A static seasonal-window note in the Pacific region configs is the manifesto-aligned shape.

### Do not adopt

- **Do not** encode *Te Ao Māori*, *moʻolelo*, *Songlines*, *Tjukurpa* (the *Anangu* law / *Dreaming*), *joik* repertoires, Sakha *Olonkho* epics, or any cosmological frame into Bios's diagnostic logic. These are not biomarker domains and are not Bios's to represent.
- **Do not** build a closed enum of Indigenous-practitioner role names. The owner provides the label; Bios records it.
- **Do not** attempt to automate detection of *karakia*, *hoʻoponopono*, ceremony, or sacred-context. The owner sets the context; Bios does not infer.
- **Do not** model *whakapapa*, *moʻokūʻauhau*, or *gáfa* (ancestral-genealogical) information without explicit collaboration with Te Mana Raraunga, NACCHO, the Pacific Health Research and Ethics Council, and the Sámi Council. This is a Tier-C concern because the feature does not exist; if it ever moves toward existing, the consultation is mandatory.
- **Do not** ship Pacific-NCD content that uses the word "epidemic" or culturally-violent language. The framing must be Pacific-authored.
- **Do not** assume Aboriginal-Australian / Torres Strait Islander / Māori / Pacific / Sámi consent on aggregated data without the relevant collective-governance body's involvement. Bios's local-first architecture means this is already the structural default; the audit asks it be preserved deliberately as features expand.

## Summary

Bios viewed through Oceanic and Arctic Indigenous-medicine frames is, at the level of architecture, **closer to Te Mana Raraunga / CARE / OCAP / *Closing the Gap* data-sovereignty principles than its documentation acknowledges** — and the documentation gap is the single cheapest fix in the audit. At the level of content, the highest-leverage clinical fit is the **Pacific NCD epidemic** (where Bios's longitudinal biomarker frame plus its already-CI-enforced no-shame language register is unusually well-aligned), the highest-priority cultural-framing change is **Te Whare Tapa Whā disclosure in the NZ region** (Bios is one wall of a four-walled house, and this fact is currently implicit-and-unstated), and the most clinically-integrated traditional-medicine precedent on the entire panel is **Ngangkari practice in the South Australian state mental-health system** (the natural framing is *legible companion, not replacement*). The Te Reo Māori / ʻōlelo Hawaiʻi / Pitjantjatjara / Yolŋu Matha / Samoan / Tongan / Fijian / Sámi / Sakha localisation gap is real and partially legally-mandated in the NZ and Hawaiʻi contexts. Bios's existing reproductive-domain isolation is the structural precedent for a generalised tapu-flagged-context mechanism. The orthogonalities — divination, *mapan*, *whakapapa* reading, *noaidi* drum-trance diagnostic — are orthogonalities not deficiencies, and the audit names them so they are not silently glossed. The instrument-not-coach posture, the silence-is-a-feature posture (*dadirri*, *whakarongo*), and the personal-baseline-not-population-norm posture are good company across all nine practitioner frames. Bios did not build for this catchment; it built for personal autonomy, and the architecture landed in the same place.
